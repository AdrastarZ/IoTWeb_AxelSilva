Important Works

1. When I Was Your Man (Album: Unorthodox Jukebox)

One of Bruno Mars's most streamed songs globally. This emotional piano ballad reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and showcases his vocal power and songwriting depth, becoming one of the best-selling digital singles in history.

2. Just the Way You Are (Album: Doo-Wops & Hooligans)

His debut solo single from the album Doo-Wops & Hooligans that launched him into global stardom. It won the Grammy Award for Best Male Pop Vocal Performance and has billions of streams worldwide.

3. That's What I Like (Album: 24K Magic)

Featured on his hit album 24K Magic, this song blends funk and R&B. It won Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards and remains one of his most listened-to tracks on every platform.
