Biography

Born and raised in Honolulu, Hawaii, Mars grew up in a musical family. His father, Peter Hernandez, is of Puerto Rican and Ashkenazi Jewish descent, while his mother, Bernadette San Pedro Bayot, was of Filipino and Spanish ancestry. Mars was nicknamed "Bruno" by his father due to his resemblance to professional wrestler Bruno Sammartino. As one of six siblings, he was exposed to a variety of musical genres, including Elvis Presley, Michael Jackson, and The Isley Brothers.

Mars began performing at a young age, joining his family’s band and gaining attention as an Elvis Presley impersonator. He made his on-screen debut in the 1992 film Honeymoon in Vegas. After graduating from President Theodore Roosevelt High School, Mars relocated to Los Angeles to pursue a music career.

Important Facts

  • Birth Name: Peter Gene Hernandez (born October 8, 1985 in Honolulu, Hawaii).

  • Multi-Instrumentalist: He plays drums, guitar, piano, bass, and ukulele in his studio recordings and live shows.

  • Songwriter & Producer: Before his solo debut, he co-founded the production team "The Smeezingtons", writing hits for other famous artists.

  • His Band: He always performs live alongside his full band, The Hooligans.

Highlights

  • Bruno Mars won 6 Grammys the same night in all the categories he was nominated.

  • One of his latest songs "I Just Might" has been on the No. 1 spot of R&B/Hip-Hop Songs Billboard chart for over 31 weeks, making it the longest time a song has been on the No. 1 spot on that chart.
