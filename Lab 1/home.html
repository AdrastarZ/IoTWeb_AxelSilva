Bruno Mars

Bruno Mars (born Peter Gene Hernandez, October 8, 1985) is a Grammy Award-winning American singer-songwriter, record producer, and performer known for his wide-ranging musical style that blends pop, R&B, funk, soul, reggae, and rock. Mars has achieved global recognition for his chart-topping hits, dynamic live performances, and creative collaborations, solidifying his status as one of the most influential artists of the 21st century.
